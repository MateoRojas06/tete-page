Just as a note, this one is a rant, no advice to be found here unless you think you can get something out of my ramblings.

The issue

So, you may have noticed that I'm not that much of an artist. I'm quite proud of the visual identity of this site, yet I can't bring myself to say it's finished.

I draw ocasionally, but I suffer from the typical issue of not seeing myself as good enough to be put out there. I had the idea of drawing an anime girl type character as some sort of site mascot, but I'm not qualified to meet my own standards on what she would look like. Of course I could keep going and get better, but how long will it take to be happy with myself? That question eats away at me.

Why is it so easy to give up?

I draw ocassionally, the reality is that I've given up on it more times than I can count. I've also given up on writting and these blogs are my way of picking it back up. The truth is that I want to be able to say I'm good at something but the process of becming good is extremely painful.

I think giving up is something that comes naturally to people like me. I also gave up smoking and alcohol without a lot of effort which would mean that this trait isn't entirely negative... Yet I feel as if it has robbed me of so many opportunities through the years

I know what I must do to feel better, I have to keep pushing forward. I don't believe in talent, i know in my heart that effort gets rewarded, not equally for all but it does. So why? Why can't I follow my own advice? I should be picking up my pencil but I feel like drawing another box or cylinder or some shit will just make me go insane.

What is it for?

What is my objective with this? Well it's something that I haven't been able to find despite the enormous ammount of art tutorials on the internet and artists I personally know

I'm even a little ashamed to admit it but, you know how sometimes people get bored and draw a cute girl in a couple of minutes? Yeah that's what I want to be able to do.

I thought it would be a comparatively easy task, I didn't have to learn about colors or composition, I thought: "Well I just have to find my style and learn to apply perspective on bodies and soon all my notebooks will be full of cute drawings." I was so very wrong, might take me a another couple of months before I'm able to draw something that I'm not directly staring at, then another few weeks to understand hair, maybe a year before I can draw clothing and more before I can be happy.

What for? So I can have a cute mascot character? It almost sounds like a joke.

Closing

I'm not sure if I enjoy drawing. It seems like I suffer from a toxic mindset where I only see drawing as a tool to get what I want. Of course that would turn the entire process of being able to draw that finished piece into a nightmare where my only escape would be getting good enough to make what I want and never draw again.

I don't want it to be like that, still, I'm the one in control of my life so I have to find the way out of it myself.

Anyways, that's pretty much all, I might learn to make more things with vectors, if I do, I'll make a section on this site so you can download free-use backgrounds. No promises tho.
